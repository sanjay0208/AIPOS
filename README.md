# 📖 Overview

AIPOS (AI Personal Operating System) is an AI-native platform that helps people organize, understand, and automate their digital life.

Unlike traditional AI chatbots, AIPOS combines long-term memory, intelligent planning, knowledge management, and proactive assistance into one unified platform.

---

# 🌟 Vision

Build the world's first AI Operating System that becomes every user's trusted digital companion.

---

# 🎯 Mission

Enable people to spend less time managing technology and more time achieving meaningful goals.
